CRITIC REVIEWS

Chicken Dum Biryani

9.9
storefront Bawarchi Biryani — RTC X Roads, Hyderabad

Slow-cooked in dum style with long-grain basmati rice, tender marination, saffron, and aromatic spices.

PROS
  • Flavorsome rice layering
  • Melt-in-mouth chicken
CONS
  • Very heavy portion

Flavor Breakdown

The rice grains are perfectly separated and infused with saffron and ghee. The masala blend reaches deep into the chicken, giving every mouthful a rich, savory aroma without being overly oily.

Aroma 10/10
Tenderness 9.8/10
Spice Level 9.5/10

Saoji Mutton Curry

9.8
storefront Saoji Bhojanalaya — Nagpur, Maharashtra

Fiery Maharashtrian curry simmered slow with black spices, coconut, and tender bone-in goat meat.

PROS
  • Intense spice punch
  • Rich gelatinous gravy
CONS
  • High spice tolerance needed

Flavor Breakdown

A legendary dish for lovers of heat. The roasted masala paste coats every piece of mutton, yielding a savory, dark, and complex flavor that pairs perfectly with bhakri or roti.

Gravy Depth 10/10
Heat 9.9/10
Authenticity 10/10
